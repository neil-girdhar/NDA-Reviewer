## NOMENCLATURE RULES FOR NDA FILENAMES

### Confidentiality Agreements (NDAs)
**Format**: `DIV-BU-REG-EntityAbbr-CustName-EffDate`

**Example**: `MNDA-MLS-ID-NA-DKUS-Customer-5Jun25`

**Breakdown**:
- **MNDA** = Mutual NDA (or NDA for one-way)
- **MLS** = Division abbreviation
- **ID** = Business Unit abbreviation  
- **NA** = Region (EMEA/APAC/AMER or NA for North America)
- **DKUS** = Entity Abbreviation (shortened form of MLSIDAMCA for D&K Engineering, Inc.)
- **Customer** = Customer name (abbreviations acceptable)
- **5Jun25** = Effective date

### Customer Contracts (Non-NDAs)
**Format**: `(Type)-DIV-BU-REG-EntityAbbr-CustName-EffDate`

**Types**:
- **MSA** = Master Supply Agreement
- **MEA** = Master Equipment Agreement
- **MSevA** = Master Services Agreement

**SOW Format** (if applicable): `(Type)-SOW([SalesForce#])-DIV-BU-REG-EntityAbbr-CustName-EffDate`

**Examples**:
- `MSA-TMS-AI-AMER-AIAM1-Boeing-15Mar25`
- `MEA-SOW(SF12345)-MLS-AX-AMER-MLSAAMMN-Medtronic-01Apr25`
